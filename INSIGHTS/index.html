    Business insights

    Index of the business questions answered by the marts schema. Each finding links to a detail file with the exact query and chart.

    About the data

    The underlying rows are CSV snapshots of the ten source tables (contracts/registry.json: 5 MySQL, 2 MongoDB, 3 Databricks public) shipped under data/raw/ and exported from the live sources by scripts/export_raw_csv.py; a clone loads them with make load-raw-csv. See the README's "About the data" section for the full description.

    • Origin: unverified. No generator or seed script exists anywhere in this repository or its git history, so the original source of the rows cannot be determined from the repo. TODO(owner): confirm whether the data was generated, taken from a public dataset, or is real.
    • Looks generated. The shipped CSVs show sequential integer keys, customerN@mailbank.com emails and branch names like Pune Branch 1 — patterns typical of generated data (observed in the files, not proof).
    • Not a benchmark. The fraud rate (0.499%), late-payment rate (12.01%) and value concentration (22.55% in the top 10% of customers) are properties of this dataset only, not real-world banking figures. Treat every finding below as a demonstration of analytical method on synthetic-looking data, not as a finding about any real bank.

    Segment views

    Each result is reproducible with the linked SQL against banking_delta.

    Domain Segment result So what SQL
    Customers Upper-Mid (36.12%) and High (32.88%) income bands hold 69.00% of transaction value from 32,786 of 47,640 active customers; average value is flat at 250,807-255,284 across every band. Target the value decile, not the income tier: average customer value barely moves by income, so income-based offers would over-serve low-value customers in high bands. customer_segments.sql
    Loans Late-payment rate is flat by loan size: 11.88% (<1L) to 12.04% (1-5L), a 0.16pp spread; loan types span 11.85-12.19%. Run reminders and collections across the whole book; there is no small-ticket or large-ticket segment to prioritise. loan_segments.sql
    Card fraud Fraud rate is 0.479% (Platinum) to 0.507% (Classic), a 0.028pp spread; Debit cards (tier N/A) carry the largest fraud amount (15,673,144.60). Tier-based blocking would miss most fraud; keep velocity and amount rules running on all tiers, especially Debit. card_fraud_segments.sql
    Transactions Value is 26.76% North, 24.15% South, 23.29% West, 15.92% Central, 9.88% East - a 2.7x North-to-East spread, while the six channels stay within 0.08pp. Set capacity, pricing and marketing by region; channel mix is too even to justify shifting traffic for share reasons. transaction_segments.sql
    Branches West branches carry the highest load (65.38 accounts per employee) against North (53.11); regional late-payment rates stay within 11.96-12.14%. Rebalance staff toward West and Central offices; do not build region-specific collections playbooks from a 0.18pp late-rate spread. branch_segments.sql
    Support Resolution is 78.61% for Account Statement to 81.15% for Net Banking Issue; satisfaction stays 2.97-3.04 across issue types, and the Low income band resolves worst (78.36%). Fix the statement, KYC and loan-query workflows first, and investigate why satisfaction does not move with resolution - the driver looks systemic, not issue-specific. support_segments.sql
